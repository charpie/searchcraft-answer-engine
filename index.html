Under the hood

Engineered for retrieval performance

Built in Rust. Designed for AI workloads. Every feature exists to get the right content to your model, faster.

⚡ Sub-millisecond retrieval

Hundreds of millions of records at speeds that make your LLM the bottleneck — not your retrieval layer.

🔍 Semantic Control Layer

Intent detection and semantic understanding without embeddings. Fully tunable, fully transparent scoring you can explain and debug.

⬡ Federated retrieval

Query across multiple content sources in a single call. Combine knowledge bases, help centers, and product docs into one unified context.

⚙ Decoupled tuning

Adjust relevancy weights, field boosting, time decay, and synonyms in real-time. No reindexing. No redeployments.

Clean, typed JSON with field-level data your LLM can trust. Not raw text blobs — structured context that reduces token waste.
